What’s new at Marquisa

The briefing now stays closer to the moment of flight

We keep refining Marquisa to make preparation clearer, faster, and closer to real operations.

In this update, the briefing gets a more objective runway reading and visuals that change with the aerodrome’s local time of day.

Runway reading

A runway-end suggestion built for the current METAR

Instead of comparing wind and runway by hand, the briefing now highlights the end with the best headwind component and shows, side by side, what matters: headwind, crosswind, and the overall wind condition.

The goal is less friction in preparation — without replacing ATIS, ATC, NOTAMs, or the pilot’s judgment.
