MARQUISA — What’s new

The briefing now stays closer to the moment of flight

We keep refining Marquisa to make preparation clearer, faster, and closer to real operations.

In this update, the briefing gets a more objective runway reading and visuals that change with the aerodrome’s local time of day.

RUNWAY READING
A runway-end suggestion built for the current METAR.
The briefing highlights the end with the best headwind component and shows headwind, crosswind, and wind condition — without replacing ATIS, ATC, NOTAMs, or pilot judgment.

Example: RWY 27 · headwind +12 kt · crosswind 0 kt · wind 270/12

BRIEFING ATMOSPHERE
The look changes with the time of day: dawn, day, dusk, night, and late night.
